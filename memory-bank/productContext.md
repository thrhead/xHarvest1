# Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
Farming operations heavily depend on weather conditions and precise crop growth stages. Traditional calendars fail when unexpected rain, heavy wind, or sudden temperature drops make pesticide spraying or soil fertilization ineffective or hazardous.

This application bridges crop knowledge (templates from Payload CMS) with real-time micro-location weather data (Open-Meteo) to provide an adaptive field task list that adjusts automatically.

## Problems Solved
- **Wasted Agrochemicals**: Prevents spraying pesticides right before heavy rain, saving cost and environmental damage.
- **Forgotten Agricultural Tasks**: Sends timely notifications for critical fertilization and irrigation windows.
- **Complex Field Tracking**: Centralizes field locations, soil conditions, and active crops in a single mobile interface.

## User Experience Goals
- **Field-Friendly UI**: High-contrast, clean layout readable under direct sunlight.
- **Automated Weather Shield**: Automatic task rescheduling with clear explanations (e.g., "Shifted due to predicted 12mm rain").
- **Offline Reliability**: Local data persistence so farmers can view tasks even without internet access in remote fields.
